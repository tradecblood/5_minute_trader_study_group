⭐ Your exact workflow (and YES — this is correct)

1️⃣ You identify a bullish trend
You see price pushing upward with momentum.

2️⃣ You spot a candle you want to enter on
This candle is your impulse candle — the one that shows strength.

But instead of entering immediately…

3️⃣ You wait for the next candle
This prevents chasing and gives you a pullback opportunity.

4️⃣ You draw Fibonacci on the candle you wanted to enter on
This candle becomes your A → B leg:

- A = low of the impulse candle  
- B = high of the impulse candle

This is the correct way to anchor Fib when using the previous candle method.

5️⃣ You watch the next candle pull back
You’re waiting for price to retrace into your Fib levels.

6️⃣ If price reaches the 0.5 level, you do NOT enter yet
You wait for confluence:

- rejection wick  
- bullish engulfing  
- break of micro‑structure  
- volume shift  
- trend continuation signal  

7️⃣ Once confluence appears, you enter on the next candle
This is the safest, most mechanical way to trade the 0.5 retracement.
